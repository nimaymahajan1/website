Atmospheric Science Research

Nimay Mahajan NSF GRFP Fellow · PhD Student, Atmospheric Sciences

Nimay is a PhD student in Atmospheric Sciences at the Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric, and Earth Science, University of Miami. He recently completed a B.S.M.A.S. in Meteorology and Mathematics, also at UM, before continuing on to the PhD.

His research applies machine learning to subseasonal-to-seasonal forecasting, with a broader interest in climate resilience and how atmospheric research can support communities facing climate-related risk.
